Hi. I’m Raygen.

Making things is the thread through most of my life. Sometimes that means a model in Onshape and a machine full of motors. Sometimes it means Svelte, TypeScript, and a tool that makes somebody’s day a little easier.

I’m also a student at One Stone, a point of contact for our makerspace, and someone who cares about helping other people get their own ideas working.

This is a home for all of that. The machines, the software, and the people I get to make things with.

The makerspace.

One Stone · Boise
2025–2027

I’m the student point of contact for the makerspace at One Stone. I develop and teach student safety protocols, and teach people how to use the tooling.

That work sits right alongside my own projects. A workshop is a place to make things, but it’s also a place to learn how—and I care about being someone other students can turn to.

Getting unstuck, together.

Assistant · Hack Club — Outpost
San Francisco · June–September 2026

At Outpost, I helped more than twenty teenagers design, engineer, and debug their hardware projects during the hackathon.

I also resolved more than half of the submitted help-desk queries. It was another side of making: understanding somebody else’s problem, working through it with them, and helping their project move forward.
