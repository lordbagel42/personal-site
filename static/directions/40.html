That takes different shapes. Sometimes it's a 3D printer. Sometimes it's software that makes a hardware project easier to finish. Sometimes it's helping another person get unstuck.

I'm very at home in Onshape, I know my way around electronics, and Hack Club is where I pour my heart and soul every day.
