Raygen Rupe
Engineer. Maker. Printer person.

I love taking an idea far enough that it becomes a real thing. 3D printers are a particular obsession. I spend a lot of time in Onshape, and I'm comfortable crossing over into electronics when the idea calls for it.

Hack Club is where I pour my heart and soul every day. It also led me to two weeks with the Stardance hardware team, helping with the tools behind the work.

A compact CoreXY printer designed in Onshape, with servo motors and CPAP cooling. Use “Look inside” in the object field to take the panels away and see the assembly.

DAVID

A Delteron 3D printer with three vertical linear rails and articulated arms. A different answer to the same irresistible question: how do you build a machine that makes things?

Two weeks at Stardance

An internship with the Hack Club Stardance hardware team. My contributions included review and certification tools, keyboard shortcuts, and timelapse tools.
