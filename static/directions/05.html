DAVID is a Delteron 3D printer. Three vertical linear rails and articulated arms give this machine its distinctive shape.

Building printers is one of my favorite ways to bring mechanical design and electronics together.

What makes DAVID different?

SERV0 uses a compact CoreXY layout. DAVID’s Delteron design uses three vertical linear rails connected to articulated arms.
