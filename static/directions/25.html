A compact CoreXY printer, with servo motors and CPAP cooling. Designed in Onshape.

What’s inside

Servo motors drive the CoreXY motion system. CPAP cooling supplies the part cooling. Use the panel control above to see the assembly under the enclosure.

DAVID

A different way to move: a Delteron 3D printer with three vertical linear rails and articulated arms.

A closer look at the motion

The three vertical rails and articulated arms define DAVID’s Delteron arrangement. The tall form makes that motion system the centerpiece.
