There’s a person
in all of this.

I love making things. That can mean getting lost in a parametric model, working through firmware, or changing a little piece of software.

It can also mean helping someone else get unstuck. Teaching a tool. Debugging an idea together. Taking care of the space where the next thing might happen.

I want this little corner of the internet to have room for all of it. The machines, the people, and the things I’m still figuring out.

— Raygen
