SERV0Small machine. A lot going on.

Building it from
the inside out.

SERV0 is my open-source, custom Voron V0.2 variant: a compact CoreXY with servo motors, steel panels, and CPAP cooling.

I designed it in Onshape and integrated custom firmware for servo control. It made it out of the workshop to Open Sauce 2026.

What holds my attention: the whole system, from a CAD model to a machine that moves.

DAVIDThree rails. Another way to move.

A different
kind of motion.

DAVID uses a Delteron, or Colinear Tripteron, motion system: three vertical rails connected to articulated arms.

I custom-designed and manufactured this open-source printer in just under three weeks, then exhibited it at Rocky Mountain RepRap Festival 2026.

Monstrosity is an open-source 3D printer with double h-bot kinematics, developed in September–October 2025.

The first iteration came together in a 24-hour sprint, followed by another 12 hours to finalize it.
