Hi, I’m Raygen.
Engineer. Student. Maker.

Making things is the thread that runs through pretty much everything I do.

Sometimes that means a machine in Onshape. Sometimes it’s TypeScript, a hardware problem, or helping someone figure out a tool they haven’t used before.

At One Stone, I’m the student point of contact for the makerspace. I develop and teach safety protocols and show other students how to use the tooling. Being able to make something is exciting. Helping someone else get there matters to me, too.

Before that, I managed the VEX Robotics Competition shop at Centennial High and helped bring the team to a local competition win. Different places, different tools. Still making things together.
