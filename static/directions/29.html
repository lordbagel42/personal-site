Hello, I’m Raygen.

I like taking an idea seriously enough to make it. Sometimes that means a printer, with every part worked out in Onshape. Sometimes it means a small software change that makes something easier to use.

And sometimes the most useful thing I can do is help someone else make their thing. At One Stone, I’m the student point of contact for the makerspace. I develop and teach safety protocols, and help students learn the tooling.

Those don’t feel like separate interests to me. They’re all ways of making an idea a little more possible.
