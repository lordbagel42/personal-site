Making things is the core of who I am. Sometimes that means a printer taking shape in Onshape. Sometimes it's software, electronics, or helping someone work through a problem.

I'm a student at One Stone, where my place in the makerspace goes beyond my own projects. I teach tooling and help students learn to work safely. The things I know become more useful when someone else can use them, too.

There's a lot I want to build. And, eventually, a lot I want to write about here. For now, this is a small collection of the work and people I put my energy into.

I love building 3D printers. Different ways of moving, different problems to untangle. All of these are open source.

SERV0

SERV0

A custom take on the Voron V0.2. Compact CoreXY motion, servo motors, steel panels, and CPAP cooling. Designed in Onshape, with custom firmware integration for servo control.

I brought it to Open Sauce in 2026.

Hack Club is where I pour my heart and soul every day. Building is a big part of it. So is being there for the people doing the building.

At Outpost, that meant helping more than twenty teenagers design, engineer, and debug their projects during the hackathon. It also meant resolving more than half of the submitted helpdesk queries.

Part of my work with the Outpost team in 2026. A place to put what I know to use, and help somebody else's idea move forward.
