Hello, I’m Raygen.

Making things is what drives me. Sometimes that means a machine with an unusual way of moving. Sometimes it means a small software change that makes someone else’s day easier. I like being involved all the way from an idea to something that works.

A lot of that happens in Onshape, working through the details of a mechanism. But there’s another side of making that matters just as much to me: helping somebody else get started. At One Stone, I’m the student point of contact for our makerspace. I develop and teach safety protocols and help students learn the tooling.

Before that, at Centennial, I was given the freedom to manage our VEX Robotics Competition shop. We brought home a local competition win. The work included building the robot and taking responsibility for the space and the team around it.

Hack Club is where I pour my heart and soul every day. Through Outpost and Stardance, I’ve helped other teenagers work through hardware problems and contributed to the software behind their projects. My own machines are a big part of this site. The people I get to make things alongside are a big part of me.

Thanks for taking a look around. I hope you find something here that makes you want to make something, too.

— Raygen

DAVID

Three rails.
A different possibility.

A Delteron, or Colinear Tripteron, printer. Three vertical rails and articulated arms create an entirely different motion system to figure out. I custom-designed and manufactured it in just under three weeks, then exhibited it at Rocky Mountain Rep Rap Festival 2026.
