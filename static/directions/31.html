I love making things. Sometimes that means an assembly in Onshape, or a printer that finally exists beyond the screen. Sometimes it means helping another person find their way into making.

I’m still a student. At , I’m the student point of contact for the makerspace. I develop and teach our safety protocols, and help students learn the tooling. Learning and teaching fit into the same day.

There’s a thread back to at Centennial. I had the autonomy to manage it, and our team came away with a local competition win. Making has always involved other people, too.

I work in software as well — Svelte and TypeScript are part of my toolkit. At , that meant contributing to timelapse and hardware flows with a team of dozens.

And at , it meant helping teenagers design, engineer, and debug their projects. I care about the thing being made. I care about the person making it, too.
