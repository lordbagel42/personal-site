Leave the
door open.

At One Stone, I’m the student point of contact for the makerspace. I develop and teach safety protocols and help students learn how to use the tooling.

Knowing your way around a workshop is useful. Helping someone else find their way around it matters to me, too.

Before One Stone, I was given autonomy to manage Centennial High’s VEX Robotics Competition shop. I helped bring our team to a local competition win.

A whole lot
of Hack Club.

Hack Club is where I pour my heart and soul every day. I care about what other people are building, and I like being there when an idea needs another pair of hands.

At Outpost, I helped more than twenty teenagers design, engineer, and debug their projects during the hackathon.

As an Outpost assistant in San Francisco, I also resolved more than half of the submitted helpdesk queries. Some of the work is making a thing. Some of it is helping someone get unstuck.
